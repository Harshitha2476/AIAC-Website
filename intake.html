ADMISSIONS // 2026 COHORT

Student Intake & Evaluation

Admission is open to early-semester undergraduate engineering students. Induction into active squads is determined entirely by the 48-hour builder evaluation.

Prerequisites

  • • Foundational Python / C proficiency
  • • Basic Git & command-line comfort
  • • 8–10 dedicated hours per week commitment
  • • Interest in one of the 6 active Research Pods

Selection Flow

  1. 1. Submit intake form & GitHub profile
  2. 2. Complete 48-hour screening task
  3. 3. 10-minute technical review call
  4. 4. Induction into 3-week bootcamp
  5. 5. Placement into Research Pod squad
