The 5 Engineering Principles

Rigorous quality gates enforced on all laboratory branches and deliverables.

MANDATORY PROTOCOL
PRINCIPLE 01
Mandatory Peer Reviews

Every branch merge requires approval from the Pod Anchor and at least one Cross-Pod Council lead.

PRINCIPLE 02
Automated CI Gates

Strict formatting with Ruff and Black, static typing with Mypy, and zero-failing Pytest runs before PR review.

PRINCIPLE 03
Deterministic Seeds

All stochastic processes (data splits, model weights, token sampling) must fix random seeds (default 42).

PRINCIPLE 04
Hardware Constraints

All model evaluations record peak VRAM usage, inference latency on standard CPUs/GPUs, and FLOP estimates.

PRINCIPLE 05
Transparent Lineage

Datasets and fine-tuned checkpoints document open licenses, source URLs, and SHA256 checksums.

GOVERNANCE
Cross-Pod Audits

The Documentation & Reproducibility Council performs random replication audits on sprint deliverables.
